# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS with Three.js (3D) and GSAP ScrollTrigger (scroll motion), no build step. Chosen by the user. Deploys by uploading the folder (Netlify, Vercel, cPanel) and must also run inside a sandboxed preview host that blocks data: fetches, external images and non-allowlisted hosts.

## Users

Brazilian entrepreneurs and small-business owners (Portuguese, pt-BR) who run a business day to day and want to lead better; many deal with focus/ADHD, pressure, price competition and negotiation. A smaller audience reads for faith-based leadership (youth groups, discipleship). They arrive mostly from Instagram/TikTok on a phone, decide on a book, and leave to buy on Amazon, Shopee, Mercado Livre, TikTok Shop or Hotmart.

## Product Purpose

HEB Academy is the publishing and teaching brand of author Hugo Evandro Batista. The site presents the five books and the author, and sends visitors to a store. Success: a visitor finds the book that fits their current problem and clicks a purchase link.

## Positioning

Written by an entrepreneur with ADHD who started without opportunities and built one of Brazil's largest comfort-furniture industries. Practical tools from lived experience, ethics and timeless principles; explicitly no ready-made formulas. Slogan: "Despertando Líderes Extraordinários". Motto: "Você não precisa ser perfeito. Precisa estar disposto."

## Operating Context

Visitors come from short-form video (Instagram @hebacademy_oficial, TikTok @hebacademy, Facebook). Purchase happens off-site. Each book exists as a physical edition (with an exclusive author bonus) and as an e-book.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five books: Alquimia do Sucesso (TDAH), Mente Inabalável, Negócios Incomparáveis, Negociando com Tubarões, Raízes Profundas. Real store links per book are in the current site.
- 3D models: protagonist closed book (geometry only, cover textures replaced), book stack (CC BY 4.0, Alice GameDev, credit required).
- Must not block normal scroll, must respect reduced motion, must work without WebGL.
- No text may be clipped or overlapped by the 3D object.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "HEB Academy" stays. The user asked for a total reinvention of the logo and visual identity (only the name is preserved); the old gold "HEB" serif monogram is not binding.
- The user wants the brand to feel modern, premium and "revolutionary", with motion and animated buttons; references supplied: a dark, softly lit product site with a scroll-rotating 3D device and masked headline reveals, and a bold agency site with a giant word behind a 3D object, floating chips and a coverflow carousel.
- Copy must read human, not AI-generated.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real book covers (5), author photo, old logo: from hebacademy.carrd.co, copied into assets/img.
- Real copy: slogan, institutional text, four pillars, author bio, book descriptions, all store links.
- No testimonials, sales numbers, reviews, press or prices. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the problem the reader has today, then the book that answers it.
2. Every book is one tap from a store.
3. The author's lived story is the proof; never invent other proof.
4. Motion serves orientation and delight, never at the cost of reading.
